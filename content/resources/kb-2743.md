<p>Access is a construction constraint, not an afterthought. An ADU project needs a safe route for crews, excavation, concrete, framing, deliveries, debris and utility work while protecting the main home, neighboring property and public right-of-way.</p>
<section id="plan-access-before-the-contract-is-final"><h2>Plan Access Before the Contract Is Final</h2>
<p>Confirm the route equipment and materials will take, the width and height restrictions, gates or fencing to be removed, and any overhead or underground obstacles. A narrow side yard can change the construction method, duration and cost.</p>
</section>
<section id="staging-and-deliveries"><h2>Staging and Deliveries</h2>
<p>Decide where materials, dumpsters, portable toilets and equipment will be placed. The plan should address street parking, driveways, sidewalks, emergency access, delivery timing and restoration of lawns, paving, fencing and irrigation.</p>
</section>
<section id="neighbor-impacts"><h2>Neighbor Impacts</h2>
<p>Construction can affect parking, noise, dust, visual privacy and temporary access. Give neighbors a practical heads-up, avoid promises the project team cannot control, and provide a single contact path for immediate site concerns.</p>
</section>
<section id="foundation-tip"><h2>Foundation Tip</h2>
<p>Walk the proposed access and staging route with the contractor before mobilization. A route that works on paper may not work once trucks, gates, slopes, utilities and occupied-home access are considered.</p>
</section>
<section id="how-foundation-can-help"><h2>How Foundation Can Help</h2>
<p>Foundation can help homeowners identify early access, staging and neighbor-impact questions so they can be addressed before construction begins. The contractor remains responsible for means, methods, safety and the site-specific logistics plan.</p>
</section>
<section id="important-disclaimer"><h2>Important Disclaimer</h2>
<p>This article provides general planning information, not construction-safety, permitting, traffic-control or contractor advice.</p>
<p>Confirm project-specific requirements with the contractor and applicable agencies.</p>
</section>
