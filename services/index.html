I'm Just Getting Started

Strategy Session™

Clarify goals, priorities, budget assumptions, risks, and next steps before early decisions become harder to change.

Who it is for

Homeowners considering a significant renovation, aligning competing priorities, or needing to regroup around an unclear scope.

When it is useful

Before committing to a design or construction direction, while considering team roles, or whenever assumptions need structure.

What Foundation does

Leads a focused conversation about goals, scope, budget assumptions, timing, risks, team roles, and tradeoffs without selecting professionals or prescribing one delivery method.

What you receive

You receive a written Foundation Renovation Roadmap™ summarizing priorities, risks, assumptions, unanswered questions, and next decisions.

Next step: Start with a complimentary conversation about your project.

I Have Plans

Plan Review™

Review your plans through a homeowner-focused lens before the next major commitment.

Who it is for

Homeowners with schematic, permit, or construction plans who want an independent review before bidding, approval, or a design milestone.

When it is useful

While questions can still be addressed, especially before contractor proposals or after a significant scope revision.

What Foundation does

Reviews supplied plans for alignment, apparent gaps, constructability concerns, potential budget impacts, coordination questions, and value opportunities. Foundation does not redesign, complete, stamp, or approve plans.

What you receive

You receive a written Plan Review Report™ organizing observations and questions for the appropriate project professionals.

Next step: Share where the plans are and what concerns you most.

03

I'm Collecting Bids

Bid Review™

Understand what proposals include, exclude, assume, and leave unanswered before you choose.

Who it is for

Homeowners with contractor proposals who need clarity about scope, allowances, exclusions, schedule assumptions, or value.

When it is useful

After contractors price substantially consistent information and before selection or contract execution.

What Foundation does

Organizes proposals into comparable categories, highlights differences and assumptions, and identifies follow-up questions. Foundation does not endorse, negotiate with, or select a contractor.

What you receive

You receive a written comparison organizing scope, allowances, exclusions, schedule assumptions, and contractor follow-up questions.

Next step: Discuss your proposals and the information used to prepare them.

Construction Has Started

Construction Support™

Get periodic, independent coaching for questions and decisions during construction.

Who it is for

Homeowners with an active renovation who need help understanding a change order, progress update, schedule question, or significant decision.

When it is useful

At selected milestones, before responding to a material change, or when project information is difficult to evaluate.

What Foundation does

Reviews homeowner-supplied information, explains options and tradeoffs, and organizes questions. Foundation does not direct trades, supervise the site, control schedules, approve work or payments, or decide for you.

What you receive

Support is tailored to the questions and decisions identified for the engagement. No standard report or log is promised.

Next step: Explain what is happening and which decision is ahead.

We're Almost Done

Final Walk™

Approach the final walkthrough and closeout with clearer documentation and better questions.

Who it is for

Homeowners approaching completion or organizing visible unresolved work and closeout questions before final payment.

When it is useful

Near completion, while punch items and missing closeout information can still be documented and discussed.

What Foundation does

Walks completed areas with the homeowner, documents visible observations, and organizes closeout questions. This is not a professional home, code, permit, architectural, or engineering inspection.

What you receive

You receive written documentation of visible punch items, incomplete work, and closeout questions identified during the consultation.

Next step: Discuss the completion stage and upcoming walkthrough.
